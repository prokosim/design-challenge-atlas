**Citation:** Ronca, V.; et al. (2025). Beyond the lab: real-world benchmarking of wearable EEGs for passive brain-computer interfaces. *Brain Informatics* (2025).

## What it supports

Consumer EEG headsets (incl. Emotiv EPOC X) showed more artefacts in dynamic tasks, needed ≥ 10 s windows and discriminated workload levels only marginally. Backs [[Emotiv EPOC X]] and [[Real-time cognitive workload measurement in the field]].
