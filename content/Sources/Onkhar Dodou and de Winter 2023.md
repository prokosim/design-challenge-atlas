**Citation:** Onkhar, V.; Dodou, D.; de Winter, J.C.F. (2023). Evaluating the Tobii Pro Glasses 2 and 3 in static and dynamic conditions. *Behavior Research Methods*, 56, 4221–4238 (published online 2023, issue 2024).

## What it supports

Accuracy of wearable eye trackers degrades in dynamic conditions; Glasses 3 outperformed Glasses 2 while walking. Backs limitations in [[Tobii Pro Glasses 3]].
