**Citation:** Peysakhovich, V.; Causse, M.; Scannella, S.; Dehais, F. (2015). Frequency analysis of a task-evoked pupillary response: Luminance-independent measure of mental effort. *International Journal of Psychophysiology*, 97(1), 30–37.

## What it supports

Shows that pupil dilation as a workload indicator is confounded by luminance, and proposes a frequency-based measure less sensitive to light. Backs [[Real-time cognitive workload]] and [[Real-time cognitive workload measurement in the field]].
