**Citation:** Diarra, M.; Theurel, J.; Paty, B. (2025). Systematic review of neurophysiological assessment techniques and metrics for mental workload evaluation in real-world settings. *Frontiers in Neuroergonomics*, 6, 1584736.

## What it supports

Field workload measurement: EEG needs artefact handling and recalibration, HRV is confounded by physical effort, pupil by lighting; no method is agreed to be most reliable. Backs [[Real-time cognitive workload measurement in the field]].
