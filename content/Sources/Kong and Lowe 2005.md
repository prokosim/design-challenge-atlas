**Citation:** Kong, Y.-K.; Lowe, B.D. (2005). Optimal cylindrical handle diameter for grip force tasks. *International Journal of Industrial Ergonomics*, 35(6), 495–507.

## What it supports

Handles of 30–40 mm were rated most comfortable (≈ 19.7 % of hand length); larger diameters reduced finger force capability. Backs [[Grip force]] and [[Excessive hand force]].
