**Citation:** Hansson, G.-Å.; Balogh, I.; Ohlsson, K.; et al. (2009). Physical workload in various types of work: Part I. Wrist and forearm. *International Journal of Industrial Ergonomics*, 39(1), 221–233.

## What it supports

Electrogoniometer measurements across 43 types of work (686 workers). Wrist exposure varied strongly even within one job title — an argument for direct measurement. Backs [[Wrist deviation angle]], [[Wrist angular velocity]] and [[Electrogoniometry]].
