**Citation:** Bangor, A.; Kortum, P.; Miller, J. (2009). Determining what individual SUS scores mean: Adding an adjective rating scale. *Journal of Usability Studies*, 4(3), 114–123.

## What it supports

Maps SUS scores to adjectives from 964 participants (e.g. mean score for "Good" ≈ 71, "Excellent" ≈ 86). Backs interpretation bands in [[SUS score]].
