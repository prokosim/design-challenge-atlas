**Citation:** Tao, D.; Tan, H.; Wang, H.; Zhang, X.; Qu, X.; Zhang, T. (2019). A systematic review of physiological measures of mental workload. *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health*, 16(15), 2716.

## What it supports

Review of 91 studies: no physiological measure worked across all task types; physical activity confounds several measures; combining measures is recommended. Backs [[Physiological workload measurement]] and [[Real-time cognitive workload measurement in the field]].
