**Citation:** McAtamney, L.; Corlett, E.N. (1993). RULA: a survey method for the investigation of work-related upper limb disorders. *Applied Ergonomics*, 24(2), 91–99.

## What it supports

Defines the Rapid Upper Limb Assessment: posture scoring for arm, wrist, neck, trunk and legs, plus muscle-use and force additions, giving a grand score 1–7 with four action levels. Backs [[RULA score]], [[Wrist deviation angle]] and [[Observational ergonomic assessment]].
