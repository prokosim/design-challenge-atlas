**Citation:** Arvidsson, I.; Dahlqvist, C.; Enquist, H.; Nordander, C. (2021). Action levels for the prevention of work-related musculoskeletal disorders in the neck and upper extremities: A proposal. *Annals of Work Exposures and Health*, 65(7), 741–747.

## What it supports

Proposes a full-shift action level of 20 °/s for median wrist angular velocity (and 60 °/s for the upper arm). It is a proposal and has been debated (letter by Forsman et al., doi:10.1093/annweh/wxab075). Backs [[Wrist angular velocity]].
