**Citation:** Agostinelli, T.; Generosi, A.; Ceccacci, S.; Mengoni, M. (2024). Validation of computer vision-based ergonomic risk assessment tools for real manufacturing environments. *Scientific Reports*, 14, 27785.

## What it supports

On real factory lines, exact RULA/REBA score agreement between 2D video pose estimation and experts was low (≈ 9–34 %), risk-level agreement higher (≈ 40–80 %). Causes: occlusion, perspective, camera placement, lighting. Backs [[Video-based pose estimation]] and [[From posture data to design change]].
