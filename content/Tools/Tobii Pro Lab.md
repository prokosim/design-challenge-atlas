## What it does

Study design, recording and analysis software for Tobii eye trackers: fixation filters, areas of interest, time to first fixation, dwell time, heat maps and gaze plots. Editions for screen-based, wearable (glasses) and online webcam studies.

## Limitations

- Works with Tobii hardware only.
- Metrics depend on filter settings; mapping wearable gaze onto areas of interest still needs manual checking.
