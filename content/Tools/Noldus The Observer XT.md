## What it does

Software for coding behaviour live or from video with a user-defined coding scheme. Produces frequencies, durations and sequences of events, and inter-rater reliability. Can synchronise video with eye-tracking and physiological data.

Publicly listed prices start at about USD 2,500 (Essential edition, 2026).

## Limitations

- **Coding is manual** — the tool organises the work but does not detect or classify errors itself. This is the core of [[Automated use-error detection and classification]].
- Results depend on the coding scheme and coder reliability.
