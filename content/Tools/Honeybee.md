## What it does

Honeybee creates, runs, and visualizes daylight simulations using [Radiance](https://radiance-online.org/) and energy models using [OpenStudio](https://www.openstudio.net/) and [EnergyPlus](https://energyplus.net/).

Honeybee supports detailed daylighting and thermodynamic modeling that tends to be most relevant during mid and later stages of design. Specifically, it creates, runs and visualizes the results of daylight and radiation simulations using [Radiance](https://radiance-online.org/) and energy models using [EnergyPlus/OpenStudio](https://www.openstudio.net/). It accomplishes this by linking the [Grasshopper/Rhino](https://www.grasshopper3d.com/) CAD environment to these engines. It also serves as an object-oriented Software Development Kit (SDK) for these engines.
