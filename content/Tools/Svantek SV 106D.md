## What it does

Six-channel human vibration meter for hand-arm (ISO 5349) and whole-body (ISO 2631) measurement, compliant with the instrument standard ISO 8041-1. Used with a triaxial hand-arm accelerometer mounted on the tool handle. Successor of the SV 106A.

## Limitations

- Results depend on accelerometer mounting and position.
- Daily exposure time must usually be estimated separately.
