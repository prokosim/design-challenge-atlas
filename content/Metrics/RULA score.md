## Definition

Rapid Upper Limb Assessment score: combines postures of upper arm, lower arm, wrist, neck, trunk and legs with muscle-use and force factors into one risk score.

## Calculation

Posture bands from the method's tables → group scores → grand score 1–7.

## Targets and thresholds

Action levels: **1–2** acceptable if not held or repeated long · **3–4** investigate, change may be needed · **5–6** investigate and change soon · **7** change immediately ([[McAtamney and Corlett 1993]]).

## Validity and limits

- A screening score, not a measurement of injury risk.
- Automated scores from video agree poorly with experts on the exact score, better on the risk level ([[Agostinelli et al 2024]]).
