## Definition

The force a user's hand must exert to hold or operate a tool or control — either peak or mean over the task.

## Calculation

Measured with an instrumented handle (force transducers) or a pressure-mapping glove; compared with the user population's strength capacity.

## Targets and thresholds

[[EN 1005-3]] gives a method for reducing basic force capacity for speed, frequency and duration, and comparing it with the required force. Handle geometry matters: cylindrical handles of about 30–40 mm were most comfortable in grip tasks ([[Kong and Lowe 2005]]).

## Validity and limits

- Pressure-film sensors measure normal force only and need careful calibration.
- Measuring on the real tool in real work is hard — see [[Grip force measurement on real tool handles in the field]].
