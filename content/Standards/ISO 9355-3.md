**Ergonomic requirements for the design of displays and control actuators — Part 3: Control actuators.** Withdrawn January 2023 without an ISO replacement (parts 1 and 2 were withdrawn as well).

It covered the selection, design and location of manual control actuators — type, dimensions, travel and operating force — especially where operating errors could cause harm.

> [!tip] Use instead
> In Europe the equivalent [[EN 894-3]] is still current.
