**Ergonomics — Manual handling — Part 3: Repetitive movements and exertions of the upper limbs.** 2nd edition, published May 2026. Replaces [[ISO 11228-3 2007 edition]] (new title).

## Scope (paraphrased)

Requirements and recommendations for identifying and assessing risk factors in repetitive upper-limb work, intended to protect nearly all healthy adults. Assistive exoskeletons, pregnant workers and specific needs of persons with disabilities are outside its scope.

## What it gives the designer

A risk-assessment framework built on the classic risk factors: action frequency, **force**, **posture** (including the wrist), lack of recovery, duration and additional factors. Relevant metrics: [[Wrist deviation angle]], [[Wrist angular velocity]], [[Grip force]].
