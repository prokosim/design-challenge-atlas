**Ergonomics of human-system interaction — Part 11: Usability: Definitions and concepts.** 2nd edition (2018), reconfirmed 2023. Replaced ISO 9241-11:1998.

## Scope (paraphrased)

Provides a framework for understanding usability as an *outcome*: how well specified users reach specified goals in a specified context of use. It applies to systems, products and services, and deliberately does not prescribe design or evaluation methods.

## What it gives the designer

A shared vocabulary that turns "is it usable?" into three measurable dimensions:

| Dimension | Meaning (paraphrased) | Typical metrics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Effectiveness | how accurately and completely goals are achieved | [[Task success rate]], [[Use error rate]] |
| Efficiency | resources spent (time, effort, cost) relative to results | [[Task completion time]] |
| Satisfaction | how far the user's physical, cognitive and emotional responses meet needs and expectations | [[SUS score]] |

The mapping to specific metrics is common practice rather than a requirement of the standard.

## Notes

Pairs with [[ISO 25062]] (how to report usability evaluations) and ISO 9241-210 (human-centred design process).
