**Safety of machinery — Anthropometric requirements for the design of workstations at machinery.** 1st edition (2002) with two corrigenda; reconfirmed 2023. Adopted in Europe as EN ISO 14738.

## Scope (paraphrased)

Explains how to use body-measurement data to size seated and standing workstations at machinery: body space, reach and clearances during normal operation. Maintenance access and screen workstations are outside its scope.

## What it gives the designer

Dimensioning rules based on body-size percentiles (typically designing reach for small users and clearance for large users) — the reference for [[Reach distance]] to controls such as an emergency stop.
