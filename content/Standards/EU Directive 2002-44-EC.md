**Directive 2002/44/EC on the minimum health and safety requirements regarding the exposure of workers to the risks arising from physical agents (vibration).** In force; last consolidated 2019 ([consolidated text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02002L0044-20190726)).

## Scope (paraphrased)

Sets minimum duties for employers in the EU to assess, measure, reduce and monitor workers' exposure to hand-arm and whole-body vibration, including health surveillance and training.

## What it gives the designer

Legal **numeric limits** for hand-arm vibration, expressed as [[Daily vibration exposure A(8)]]:

| Value | Hand-arm A(8) |
| --- | --- |
| Exposure action value | 2.5 m/s² |
| Exposure limit value | 5 m/s² |

(Whole-body vibration has separate values.) Hand-arm assessment refers to [[ISO 5349-1]]. For a tool designer this translates into a design target: the lower the tool's vibration emission, the longer a worker may use it per day.
