**Safety of machinery — Ergonomics requirements for the design of displays and control actuators — Part 3: Control actuators.** Current in Europe (listed by BSI). Location and arrangement of displays and actuators is covered by EN 894-4:2010.

## Scope (paraphrased)

Guidance on choosing, designing and placing manual control actuators on machinery so they suit the task and the operator and minimise the risk of operating errors.

## What it gives the designer

Design requirements for actuator type, size, travel and resistance — relevant when an emergency control must be found and operated quickly ([[Poor discoverability of controls]], [[Unreachable controls]]).
