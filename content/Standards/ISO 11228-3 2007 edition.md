**Ergonomics — Manual handling — Part 3: Handling of low loads at high frequency.** Withdrawn 8 May 2026, replaced by [[ISO 11228-3]].

This note is kept so that older studies and tool claims that cite the 2007 edition stay traceable. The 2007 edition centred on the OCRA method (index and checklist) for repetitive upper-limb work.

> [!info] Why keep withdrawn standards?
> Tools and papers often claim compliance with a specific edition. Keeping the old edition as its own note — linked through `supersedes` — shows the version history and flags metrics that still point at an outdated edition.
