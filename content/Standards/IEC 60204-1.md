**Safety of machinery — Electrical equipment of machines — Part 1: General requirements.** Edition 6.0 (2016) with Amendment 1 (2021). European version EN 60204-1:2018/A1:2025. A 7th edition is in drafting (not yet published as of September 2026).

## Scope (paraphrased)

Covers the electrical, electronic and programmable electronic equipment of machines that are not hand-held during work, up to 1000 V AC / 1500 V DC. It addresses design, installation and documentation from the supply connection onwards.

## What it gives the designer

- Mostly electrical design requirements and verification tests.
- On the human side: **colour coding** of actuators and indicators. The operating part of an emergency stop device is red; where there is a background behind it, that background is yellow (consistent with [[ISO 13850]]).
- No ergonomic force or reach limits — see [[EN 1005-3]] and [[ISO 14738]] for those.
