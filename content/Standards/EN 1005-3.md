**Safety of machinery — Human physical performance — Part 3: Recommended force limits for machinery operation.** Consolidated with A1 in 2008. CEN has no public catalogue page; the link goes to BSI (also listed by NEN).

## Scope (paraphrased)

Guidance for machinery manufacturers on keeping the muscular forces operators must exert within acceptable limits. It distinguishes professional machinery (healthy working adults) from domestic machinery (general population), using European population data.

## What it gives the designer

A three-step **force limit method** in newtons: a basic force capacity for typical actions (hand grip, arm push/pull, whole-body push/pull, pedal), reduced for speed, frequency and duration, then compared with the actual required force to decide whether a design is recommended, not recommended or to be avoided. This is the reference for [[Grip force]] and actuator forces.
