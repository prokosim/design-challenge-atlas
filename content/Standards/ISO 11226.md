**Ergonomics — Evaluation of static working postures.** 1st edition (2000) with Cor 1:2006; confirmed 2018, marked *to be revised* in 2026.

## Scope (paraphrased)

Gives acceptable limits for static working postures depending on joint angles and holding time.

## What it gives the designer

Numeric angle limits for the trunk, head/neck and upper arm. For the **wrist** it gives **no numeric limits** — only the advice to avoid extreme joint positions ([[Delleman and Dul 2007]]). This is why [[Wrist deviation angle]] targets usually come from methods such as RULA or from research proposals instead.
