**Systems and software engineering — SQuaRE — Common Industry Format (CIF) for reporting usability evaluations.** 1st edition (January 2025). Replaces ISO/IEC 25062:2006, withdrawn January 2025.

## Scope (paraphrased)

Classifies types of usability evaluation and specifies what a usability evaluation report must contain, for software, hardware and services.

## What it gives the designer

A **reporting format**: participants, context, tasks and the [[ISO 9241-11]] measures — effectiveness ([[Task success rate]], [[Use error rate]]), efficiency ([[Task completion time]]) and satisfaction ([[SUS score]]). Useful when results from different studies or tools need to be comparable.
