**Mechanical vibration — Measurement and evaluation of human exposure to hand-transmitted vibration — Part 1: General requirements.** 1st edition (2001), reconfirmed 2026. Companion: **ISO 5349-2:2001 (+Amd 1:2015)** — practical guidance for measurement at the workplace ([catalogue](https://www.iso.org/standard/27511.html)). Related new part: ISO 5349-3:2025 on hand-transmitted shocks.

## Scope (paraphrased)

Part 1 defines how to measure and report vibration entering the body through the hands and how to convert it into a daily exposure value. An informative annex relates exposure to the risk of vascular symptoms (vibration white finger). Part 2 explains how to do this in real workplaces: identifying tasks, placing sensors, measurement duration and uncertainty.

## What it gives the designer

- The **vibration total value** a_hv: root-sum-of-squares of the frequency-weighted rms acceleration in three axes (m/s²).
- The **daily exposure** A(8), normalised to an 8-hour day — see [[Daily vibration exposure A(8)]].
- The measurement method referenced by [[EU Directive 2002-44-EC]].
