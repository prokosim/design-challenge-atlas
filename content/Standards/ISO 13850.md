**Safety of machinery — Emergency stop function — Principles for design.** 3rd edition (2015), replaced ISO 13850:2006. Marked *to be revised* in 2026; a new draft is in preparation.

## Scope (paraphrased)

Sets functional requirements and design principles for the emergency stop function on machinery, independent of the energy source. It does not cover machines where an emergency stop would not reduce risk, nor hand-held or hand-guided machines. Electrical implementation is left to [[IEC 60204-1]].

## What it gives the designer

Design requirements such as: stop category, latching of the actuator, reset that must not restart the machine, suitable actuator types, and the colour convention of a **red actuator on a yellow background**.

It sets **no numeric performance targets** for the human side — e.g. how quickly an operator must be able to find and actuate the stop. Those have to come from task analysis and testing, see [[Time to first correct action]] and the gap [[Predicting emergency response time before a prototype exists]].
