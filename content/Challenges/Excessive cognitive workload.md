## Problem

The interface or task demands more attention, memory and decision-making than the operator can give — especially at peak moments such as alarms or abnormal situations.

## Design questions

- How demanding is the task compared with an alternative design?
- *When* during the task does workload peak?
- Does workload rise to a level where errors become likely?

## Why it matters

Overload leads to missed signals, slower responses and errors; underload leads to loss of vigilance. Designers need to know not only *how much* but *when* — which today is hard to measure ([[Real-time cognitive workload measurement in the field]]).
