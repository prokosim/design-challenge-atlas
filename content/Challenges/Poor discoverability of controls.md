## Problem

Users cannot find a control or function when they need it — because of its position, size, contrast, labelling or visual clutter around it.

## Design questions

- How long does it take users to look at the right control after a cue?
- Is the delay caused by *searching* (visual attention) or by *deciding/acting*?
- Do users find the control without help on the first attempt?

## Why it matters

For an emergency stop, every second of searching is a second of exposure to the hazard. Standards set conventions (red on yellow, [[ISO 13850]]) but no search-time targets.
