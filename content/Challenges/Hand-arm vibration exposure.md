## Problem

Powered hand tools transmit vibration to the hands and arms, which over years can cause vascular, neurological and musculoskeletal damage (hand-arm vibration syndrome).

## Design questions

- What vibration magnitude does the tool transmit at the grip?
- How long could a worker use it per day before reaching the EU action or limit value?

## Why it matters

Exposure is legally limited in the EU ([[EU Directive 2002-44-EC]]); lower vibration emission directly increases permitted daily use time.
