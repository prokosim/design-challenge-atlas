## Problem

Operators do not trust an automated system enough to use it (disuse) — or trust it too much and stop monitoring it (complacency).

## Design questions

- Does the operator's trust match the system's actual reliability?
- Which interface cues increase or decrease appropriate trust?

## Why it matters

Mis-calibrated trust is a known cause of incidents in automated systems.
