## Problem

Users experience a product as difficult, confusing or unpleasant to use — even when they eventually succeed.

## Design questions

- How do users rate the usability compared with benchmarks or the previous version?
- Is the perceived difficulty explained by long task times?

## Why it matters

Perceived usability drives acceptance, correct use and willingness to adopt. It is cheap to measure and comparable across studies.
