## Problem

Holding or operating a tool or control requires more hand force than users can comfortably exert, especially when repeated or sustained.

## Design questions

- How much grip force does the tool require during typical use?
- How does handle diameter and shape change the required force?
- Is the required force within recommended limits for the intended population?

## Why it matters

High forces combined with repetition and awkward postures are key risk factors for upper-limb disorders ([[ISO 11228-3]]) and exclude weaker users. Recommended force limits: [[EN 1005-3]].
