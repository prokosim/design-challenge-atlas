## Problem

The geometry of a tool or workstation forces the wrist into flexion, extension or sideways deviation, often combined with fast repetitive movement.

## Design questions

- Does the current handle geometry create excessive wrist deviation?
- How fast and how often does the wrist move during the task?
- Which design change reduces wrist deviation most?

## Why it matters

Awkward and fast wrist movements are associated with musculoskeletal disorders such as carpal tunnel syndrome. Standards give little numeric guidance for the wrist ([[ISO 11226]]), so measurement is essential.
