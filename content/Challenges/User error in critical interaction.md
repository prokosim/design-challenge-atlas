## Problem

Users perform an incorrect action — or fail to act — during an interaction where mistakes have serious consequences: stopping a machine in an emergency, setting a medical device, confirming an irreversible command.

## Design questions

- How often do users make errors on the critical task, and which step causes most of them?
- Can users identify the correct action without assistance?
- How quickly do users recover after an error?
- Can the user find and actuate the emergency control fast enough?

## Why it matters

Use errors are usually caused by the interface design, not by careless users ([[IEC 62366-1]]). In safety-related products they are a direct source of risk and a regulatory concern.
