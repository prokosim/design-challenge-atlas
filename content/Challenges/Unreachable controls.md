## Problem

A control cannot be reached — or only with an awkward stretch — by part of the user population from the normal working position.

## Design questions

- Can the smallest intended user reach the control from the normal working position?
- Is the emergency stop reachable from every position where a hazard can occur?

## Why it matters

A control that is out of reach is as good as missing, and awkward reaches add time and strain. Dimensioning principles are given in [[ISO 14738]].
