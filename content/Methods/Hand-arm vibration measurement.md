## What it is

Measuring the vibration a tool transmits to the hands with a triaxial accelerometer, following [[ISO 5349-1]] and ISO 5349-2.

## How it works

1. Mount the accelerometer on the handle where the hand grips.
2. Record frequency-weighted acceleration in three axes during representative operation.
3. Compute the vibration total value a_hv and, with the daily exposure time, [[Daily vibration exposure A(8)]].

## Strengths

- Standardised and legally recognised ([[EU Directive 2002-44-EC]]).

## Limitations

- Sensitive to mounting and position; daily exposure time is often estimated rather than measured.
