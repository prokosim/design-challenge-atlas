## What it is

A small group of evaluators inspects an interface against recognised usability principles ("heuristics") and lists violations with a severity rating.

## How it works

1. 3–5 evaluators review the interface independently.
2. Each problem is linked to a violated heuristic and rated for severity.
3. Findings are merged and prioritised.

## Strengths

- Fast and cheap; works on sketches and prototypes.

## Limitations

- Predicts problems rather than measuring them; no user data.
- Misses domain-specific problems (e.g. under stress, gloves, noise) unless evaluators know the context.
