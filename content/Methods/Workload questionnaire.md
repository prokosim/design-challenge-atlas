## What it is

Participants rate the workload they experienced immediately after a task using a standardised questionnaire — most commonly the NASA Task Load Index.

## How it works

1. After each task, the participant rates six dimensions (mental, physical and temporal demand, performance, effort, frustration).
2. Optionally, 15 pairwise comparisons give individual weights.
3. Compute the overall [[NASA-TLX score]] (weighted or raw).

## Strengths

- Validated, free, quick, works in lab and field.

## Limitations

- Subjective and retrospective — captures workload *after* the task, not the peaks during it.
- Cannot run continuously in real operations — see [[Real-time cognitive workload measurement in the field]].
