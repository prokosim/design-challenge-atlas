## What it is

A validated post-test questionnaire that measures perceived usability, such as the System Usability Scale (SUS).

## How it works

1. After the test session, participants answer the questionnaire.
2. Responses are scored with the published procedure (e.g. 0–100 for SUS).
3. Scores are compared with benchmarks.

## Strengths

- Cheap, comparable across products and studies thanks to published benchmarks.

## Limitations

- Measures perception, not performance — a product can score well and still cause critical errors.
