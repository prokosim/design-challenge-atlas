## What it is

Measuring joint angles continuously with flexible strain-gauge sensors taped across a joint — the reference field method for wrist posture.

## How it works

1. Attach a biaxial sensor across the wrist (flexion/extension and radial/ulnar deviation).
2. Record during the whole task or shift with a portable logger.
3. Report angle percentiles and angular velocity ([[Wrist deviation angle]], [[Wrist angular velocity]]).

## Strengths

- Continuous, accurate (a few degrees), works in the field.

## Limitations

- One joint per sensor; skin movement and crosstalk between axes, especially with forearm rotation.
