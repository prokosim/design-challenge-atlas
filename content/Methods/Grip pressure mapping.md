## What it is

Thin-film pressure sensors on the hand or glove measure how much force is applied, and where, while gripping a tool or control.

## How it works

1. Fit sensor regions to fingers and palm.
2. Calibrate the sensors.
3. Record while the user operates the tool; get total force, peak pressure and distribution over the hand.

## Strengths

- Shows force *distribution*, not just the total — useful for handle shape decisions.

## Limitations

- Accuracy depends strongly on calibration; measures only normal force (no shear); the sensor changes how the grip feels — see [[Grip force measurement on real tool handles in the field]].
