## What it is

An ergonomist scores working postures, forces and repetition from direct observation or video using a checklist method such as RULA or REBA.

## How it works

1. Select the worst or most frequent postures of the task.
2. Score each body region with the method's tables (e.g. wrist flexion beyond 15° scores higher in RULA).
3. Add force and repetition factors; read off the action level.

## Strengths

- Quick, cheap, widely understood; no equipment needed.

## Limitations

- Snapshot of selected postures; inter-rater variation; angles estimated by eye.
